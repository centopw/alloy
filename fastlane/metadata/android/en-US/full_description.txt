Alloy is a chat app for Matrix, the open network that lets you talk to people on any server, with end-to-end encryption. It is free and open source, and it never collects any data about you: no analytics, no tracking, no ads.

Use your existing Matrix account (matrix.org, your company's server, your own) or create one on any server.

<b>What you can do</b>
- Chat in private encrypted rooms, group rooms and direct messages.
- Reply (swipe a message), edit, delete, react with one tap, pin messages, forward, start threads, create polls, send photos, files and voice messages, and see when your messages have been read.
- Press and hold a message for a quick reaction bar and actions.
- Search your rooms, and search inside a chat for words or for who said them.
- Organise rooms with your own colour tags, favourites and archive.
- Send your location.

<b>Notifications without Google services</b>
Alloy uses UnifiedPush, so notifications arrive while the app is closed without Google Play Services. Install a UnifiedPush distributor such as ntfy, then turn it on in Settings > Notifications. Message text shows in a notification only if you allow it.

<b>Privacy</b>
- No telemetry, analytics or crash reporting.
- Private rooms use Matrix end-to-end encryption.

<b>Open source</b>
Apache-2.0. Source code, issues and releases: https://github.com/centopw/alloy
